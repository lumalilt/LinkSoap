# LinkSoap Privacy Policy

**Effective date: September 3, 2026**

**Last updated: September 3, 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how LinkSoap, provided by LumaLilt ("LinkSoap," "we," "us," or "our"), handles information when you use the LinkSoap app, its extensions, widgets, App Intents, and related support services.

## Privacy at a glance

LinkSoap is designed to clean links on your device. We do not operate an account service or a LinkSoap server that receives the links you clean, the pages you visit, your clipboard contents, your rules, or your saved links.

Some features necessarily communicate with other services:

- Opening or previewing a link contacts the destination website or the external app or browser you select.
- Optional redirect and canonical-link resolution contacts the destination website using an ephemeral network session.
- Optional Cleaning Activity sync stores aggregate counts in your private iCloud database through Apple CloudKit.
- The free version may display advertising supplied by Google Mobile Ads, subject to applicable consent and privacy choices.
- Purchases and TestFlight or App Store distribution are handled by Apple.

The sections below explain these cases in more detail.

## Information processed on your device

LinkSoap may process the following information locally to provide features you request:

- URLs and surrounding text shared with LinkSoap or supplied through Shortcuts, App Intents, widgets, or the Share Sheet;
- clipboard contents, but only after you take an action that asks LinkSoap or an installed Shortcut to read or process the clipboard;
- link-cleaning rules, allowed parameters, browser choices, feature preferences, and imported rule files;
- cleaned links you explicitly save; and
- purchase and trial status needed to make unlocked features available.

The app and its extensions share necessary local settings, rules, saved links, and entitlement state through an Apple App Group on your device. LinkSoap does not send this content to a LumaLilt analytics or link-processing service.

You control whether to share, open, copy, save, import, or export a link or rules file. Content you send to another app or service is then governed by that recipient's privacy practices.

## Cleaning Activity and iCloud

If Cleaning Activity is enabled, LinkSoap records daily totals for:

- links from which one or more parameters were removed; and
- the number of parameters removed.

Activity records do not contain the cleaned URL, destination domain, query-parameter names or values, clipboard contents, or page content.

If you enable iCloud sync for Cleaning Activity, LinkSoap stores activity dates and aggregate counts, a randomly generated device identifier, synchronization-generation identifiers, and update timestamps in the private CloudKit database associated with your Apple Account. Apple processes this information under [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). LumaLilt does not use these records for advertising, analytics, or a public leaderboard.

You can turn activity display or iCloud synchronization off in LinkSoap. Turning synchronization off stops future synchronization but may not immediately delete records already stored in iCloud. Use **Reset Cleaning Activity** in the app to reset current activity data across participating devices. You may also manage LinkSoap's iCloud data through Apple's device and account controls.

## Web previews, redirect resolution, and external apps

When you preview or open a link, the destination website, browser, or associated app may receive information normally sent with an internet request, such as your IP address, device and browser characteristics, cookies or other storage maintained by that destination, and the URL being requested. Those parties control their own collection and use of information.

LinkSoap's built-in webpage preview uses nonpersistent browser storage. Optional redirect and canonical-page resolution use an ephemeral network session and do not intentionally reuse browser cookies, credentials, or caches. Ephemeral operation reduces persistence on your device; it does not prevent the destination website or network providers from observing a request.

If LinkSoap routes a link to Safari, Chrome, Firefox, Brave, Edge, or an associated app, that app's privacy policy and settings apply.

## Advertising and consent

Unless ads have been removed through an applicable purchase, trial, or entitlement, LinkSoap may use Google Mobile Ads to display banner ads in the main app. Ads are not displayed inside LinkSoap's Share extensions.

Depending on device settings, region, consent choices, and Google's configuration, Google Mobile Ads may process information such as:

- IP address, which may be used to estimate general location;
- device or advertising identifiers;
- ad views, clicks, and other interactions;
- diagnostic information, including crash and performance data; and
- information used for ad delivery, fraud prevention, frequency limits, reporting, and measurement.

Where required, LinkSoap uses Google's User Messaging Platform to request consent or present privacy choices before requesting ads. When Google indicates that privacy choices are available, you can reopen them from **Ad Privacy Choices** in LinkSoap Settings. Non-personalized ads may still use information for functions such as fraud prevention, frequency capping, and aggregated reporting.

LinkSoap does not currently request permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. If our advertising practices materially change, we will update this policy, the app's privacy disclosures, and any required permission flow before applying that change.

For more information, see [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites) and [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## Purchases

Apple processes purchases, restores, payment details, receipts, and related account information. LinkSoap receives transaction and entitlement information needed to unlock purchased features, but LumaLilt does not receive your full payment-card information. Apple's terms and privacy practices govern App Store purchases.

## Diagnostics, TestFlight, and support

Apple may provide crash reports, performance diagnostics, and TestFlight feedback to LumaLilt when you choose to share that information through Apple. These reports may include device, operating-system, app-version, and technical diagnostic details. We use them to troubleshoot, secure, and improve LinkSoap.

If you contact us or submit a GitHub issue, discussion, security report, or other support request, we process the information you choose to provide in order to respond and investigate. GitHub and any communication provider you use process that information under their own policies. Do not post sensitive personal information, private URLs, credentials, or confidential documents in a public issue.

## Information sharing

We do not sell your personal information for money. Information may be processed or disclosed only as described in this policy, including:

- to Apple for App Store distribution, purchases, diagnostics, and optional private CloudKit sync;
- to Google for advertising, consent management, measurement, security, and fraud prevention;
- to destination websites and external apps when you ask LinkSoap to resolve, preview, share, or open a link;
- to service providers used to operate support channels; or
- when reasonably necessary to comply with law, protect rights and safety, investigate abuse, or enforce applicable terms.

Depending on your jurisdiction and the advertising configuration applicable to you, some advertising-related disclosure may be considered "sharing," "targeted advertising," or "cross-context behavioral advertising" under local law. Use the consent and privacy controls presented in the app where available, or remove ads through an applicable purchase.

## Retention and deletion

Rules, settings, and saved links remain on your device until you change or delete them, remove the app, or erase the device, subject to Apple's device backups and synchronization behavior. Cleaning Activity remains until you reset it or remove it using applicable device and iCloud controls. Apple retains purchase records and information it processes according to its own policies and legal obligations.

Support messages and voluntarily supplied diagnostics are retained only as long as reasonably needed to address the request, maintain security and project history, comply with law, or establish and defend legal claims. Public GitHub contributions may remain in repository history even after an issue or comment is edited or closed.

## Security

We use platform security features and limit LinkSoap's first-party data handling to what is needed for its features. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security for information processed by Apple, Google, destination websites, external apps, or other third parties.

Please follow the private reporting guidance in our [Security Policy](https://github.com/lumalilt/LinkSoap/blob/main/SECURITY.md) if you believe you have found a security vulnerability.

## Children

LinkSoap is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children through a LumaLilt account or backend service. LinkSoap can display websites and ads supplied by third parties. Parents and guardians should use Apple's parental controls and supervise children's web access as appropriate.

## Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights concerning personal information, including access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, portability, or withdrawal of consent. Much of LinkSoap's first-party data exists only on your device or in your private iCloud database and can be managed with the app's controls, by removing the app, or through Apple settings.

You can also:

- choose whether LinkSoap reads clipboard contents by deciding whether to invoke a paste or clipboard action;
- disable Cleaning Activity or its iCloud synchronization;
- reset Cleaning Activity;
- delete saved links and change or replace rules;
- revisit ad privacy choices when the option is available; and
- remove ads through an applicable purchase.

For a privacy question or request, use the contact route below. We may need enough information to understand and verify the request. We cannot directly retrieve data that is available only on your device or in services controlled by Apple or another third party.

## International processing

Apple, Google, GitHub, destination websites, and other services may process information in countries other than the one where you live. Their privacy policies describe the safeguards and locations applicable to their processing.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when LinkSoap's features, service providers, or legal obligations change. We will revise the **Last updated** date above and, when appropriate, provide additional notice in the app or through the project's release information.
